## How to Use

### Starting a Chat with Mia
1. Visit the homepage
2. Click on the chat input or quick-start buttons
3. Type your question or click the microphone for voice

### Configuring AI Providers
1. Go to WordPress Admin → Settings → Mia Settings
2. Add your API keys (OpenAI, Grok, Hive)
3. Select preferred provider for chat/voice

### Browsing Providers
1. Click "All Providers" in header
2. Filter by category, specialty, or location
3. View provider profiles and contact information
